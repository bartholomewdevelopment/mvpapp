What changed

Five upgrades, zero rebrand.

01

Your colours, kept

Slate-950 and slate-900 stay as the base. The coral stays the accent. Nothing new was introduced — the palette below is lifted straight from the existing CSS.

02

Coral, promoted

The coral gradient became a first-class token instead of wallpaper. It now owns the primary CTA, the flagship card's border, and one phrase per headline.

03

Type with a spine

Instrument Sans display face on a fluid scale up to 68px at 700 weight, −0.04em tracking, mono micro-labels, and body copy capped at a readable measure.

04

Depth you can feel

Coral glow on primary actions, a true gradient border on the flagship card, and darker inset wells so prices sit in a pocket instead of floating.

05

Motion with a point

Every idle pulse and CTA shimmer removed. What's left: one slow ambient drift, 140ms hovers, a spring on press, and reduced-motion fully respected.

+

Polish that ships

Skip link, visible focus rings, aria states, 44px tap targets, and a contrast-checked ink ramp — none of which existed before.

FAQ

Fluid, not stepped

Every display size is a clamp() — headings scale with the viewport instead of jumping at breakpoints.

Nav that reacts

Transparent over the hero, hairline + blur once you scroll. The menu button is a 44px target with proper aria state.

Reduced motion honoured

Users with motion sensitivity get the full page instantly — including a rule that forces reveal content visible, which the old build could leave stuck invisible.
